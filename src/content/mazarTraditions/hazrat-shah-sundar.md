Hazrat Shah Sundar Rahmatullah Alaih was a prominent spiritual luminary and saint who played a notable role in the historical propagation of Islam throughout Bengal. He was among the three hundred and sixty revered Sufi saints who accompanied the celebrated mystic Hazrat Shah Jalal Yamani Rahmatullah Alaih on his historic journey to the region. Following their arrival, he was explicitly instructed by Hazrat Shah Jalal Rahmatullah Alaih to establish his base in the coastal territory of Chittagong, where he devoted his life to guiding the local population, preaching Islamic values, and instilling spiritual light.

The sacred resting place of Hazrat Shah Sundar Rahmatullah Alaih is situated at the bustling Kotwali More in Chittagong city. Despite being located at an extremely busy urban intersection characterized by continuous noise and heavy traffic, the shrine compound remains a tranquil sanctuary. The site is meticulously maintained, preserved, and kept remarkably clean through the continuous care and respect demonstrated by local caretakers and visiting devotees.

Over the centuries, Hazrat Shah Sundar Rahmatullah Alaih has drawn deep spiritual reverence from people of diverse backgrounds and religious traditions. His shrine serves as a unique place of harmony where both Muslim and Hindu devotees gather to offer their respects, recite prayers, and express their devotion. The sanctuary accommodates all visitors by offering dedicated, separated areas for men and women to engage in quiet reflection, worship, and prayer. Visitors flock to the shrine to honor the saint’s enduring spiritual legacy, seek solace, and offer greetings in remembrance of his historical role in spreading the faith across Bengal.
