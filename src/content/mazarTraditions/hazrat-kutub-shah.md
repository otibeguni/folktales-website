Hazrat Kutub Shah was a prominent saint and traveling companion of Hazrat Shahjalal, known for his spiritual powers and role in spreading Islam across Sylhet. He arrived in Bengal alongside Hazrat Shahjalal and the three hundred and sixty saints during their historic journey to spread the Islamic faith in the region.

A central miracle associated with Hazrat Kutub Shah involves his interaction with a local Hindu king. Upon witnessing the saint's remarkable spiritual abilities and supernatural powers, the Hindu monarch was deeply moved and chose to embrace Islam. Following his conversion, the king became a follower of the saint, and Hazrat Kutub Shah played a key role in bringing many inhabitants of the region to the Islamic faith.

Today, the shrine dedicated to Hazrat Kutub Shah stands as a notable place of visitation and spiritual devotion in Sylhet. Devotees and visitors travel to the mausoleum to honor his legacy, reflect on his miracles, and pay respect to his efforts as one of the key companions of Hazrat Shahjalal.
